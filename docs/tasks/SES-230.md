# SES-230: Fly the TypeScript harvester on IHoverEngine

## Background

The TypeScript harvester flies by setting a thrust vector on the engine.
That equipment is now RCS. The main engine, IHoverEngine, thrusts only
along the nose, and the ship turns with a rotate command.

A flight planner already builds this kind of flight: turn to a direction,
then burn along the nose for a computed time. The harvester does not use
it yet. This is the fourth step of
[EPIC-1](../epics/EPIC-1/README.md).

## Require

Change the TypeScript harvester so a flight to a destination uses the
ship's rotate command and IHoverEngine, as computed by the existing flight
planner.

RCS is not used to fly those legs. Mining, unloading, and the rest of the
harvester stay in place.

## Acceptance

- On a flight, the harvester turns the ship with the rotate command, then
  thrusts with IHoverEngine along the nose.
- The turn and the burn are the ones the flight planner produces for that
  ship's mass, orientation, maximum rotation speed, and the hover engine's
  maximum thrust.
- The ship arrives and still mines and unloads afterwards.
- Those flights do not use RCS for their acceleration. They succeed while
  RCS keeps its current maximum thrust.
