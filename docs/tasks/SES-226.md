# SES-226: Resource container transfer limits come from its blueprint

## Background

A transfer is not instant, and it cannot bridge an arbitrary gap. The
sending container moves mass at a limited rate, in kilograms per second of
ingame time, and only while the gap between the two ships stays small
enough. Today both limits are the same for every container: 2000 kilograms
per second, and a gap of 200 meters. The protocol documentation states those
numbers as rules of the interface.

A client cannot ask a particular container how fast it can give resources,
or how far. A blueprint cannot make one container slower, faster, or
shorter-ranged than another. Volume says how much fits. It does not say how
the container gives that mass away.

## Require

Make these two limits properties of a resource container's blueprint, and
report both in the container's specification:

- the maximum rate at which it can give resources, in kilograms per second
  of ingame time;
- the maximum gap at which it can keep giving resources, in meters. The gap
  is the distance between the ships after each ship's radius is subtracted.

An outgoing transfer uses the sending container's own rate and its own
maximum gap. Both limits apply to the container that is giving the resource.

The protocol documentation describes both as part of the container
specification.

## Acceptance

- The specification of a container reports its maximum outgoing rate, in
  kilograms per second of ingame time, and its maximum transfer gap, in
  meters.
- Both values are the ones set on the container's blueprint. Two containers
  with different rates or different gaps report those different values.
- During a transfer, the mass that leaves the sender per second of ingame
  time stays within the sender's rate.
- The transfer continues while the gap stays within the sender's maximum
  gap, and the server stops it when the gap becomes larger.
- A container that is only receiving keeps its own outgoing rate and gap
  unused. Two senders to the same port are each limited by their own rate
  and their own gap.
- The resource container protocol documentation names both limits as fields
  of the specification.
